# Standard Two-Way Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
## RitualDev Studio

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between **RitualDev Studio** ("RitualDev", with operations at `ritualdev.in`) and the receiving / disclosing enterprise party ("Counterparty").

### 1. Purpose
The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning enterprise deployment of RitualDev's AI agent defense suite (AgentShield, DBShield, SkillPrism, private VPC clusters) and may disclose confidential business and technical information to one another.

### 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" encompasses all proprietary data, architecture topologies, pricing models, source code, vulnerability research, and security audit reports disclosed under this relationship.

### 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that:
1. Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement.
2. Was already known to the receiving party prior to disclosure without confidentiality restrictions.
3. Is independently developed without reference to the disclosing party's Confidential Information.

### 4. Zero-Data-Retention Commitment
RitualDev explicitly affirms that its technical architecture does not ingest, transmit, or retain Counterparty source code, API credentials, or prompt telemetry. All runtime execution occurs 100% locally on Counterparty machines or inside Counterparty's private air-gapped VPC infrastructure.

### 5. Term & Governing Law
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the date of mutual electronic execution. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws selected during electronic execution (State of Delaware, State of California, England & Wales, or Republic of India).
